## Open Gaps
- No method yet matches baseline on E0–E6 *and* wins E7.
- Large step recovery still dominates error budget for baseline‑safe variants.
- DDAC:step now reports poll metrics but still does not beat baseline E7 in short acceptance runs.
- Step recovery reporting now includes per‑poll‑rate sections; medians still read 0s in short sweeps when methods never reach the 1ms threshold.
- Step recovery thresholds now include 5ms/10ms; large‑jump recovery remains unresolved (see `bench/STEP_RECOVERY_SWEEP.md`).
- Clock‑jump per‑poll‑rate sweep (E45) shows large‑step dominance persists across 1/5/10 Hz.
- E53/E54 multi‑seed sweeps show StepGuard/DDAC step variants failing to recover to 1ms; recovery threshold or detection logic likely needs revision.

## Next Focus
- Probe‑only step detection under sparse traffic (E33/E39/E41/E42); probe_hard still not improving.
- Consider evaluating probe‑based StepGuard variants or alternative recovery thresholds on E45/E53 to quantify partial recovery for large jumps.
- Asymmetric drift + network pulse stress (E38) for skew‑based methods.
- Longer multi‑seed sweeps on contenders for stable ranking.
- Assess GSP step_xor/step_xor_rtt variants in multi‑seed step runs.
- Investigate step-only report sentinel/overflow values to distinguish “no recovery” vs extreme errors.
- Validate E55–E58 in broader sweeps and tune baseline‑safe thresholds for asymmetric jitter.
